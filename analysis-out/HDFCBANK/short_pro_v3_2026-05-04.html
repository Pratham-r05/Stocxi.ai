HDFC Bank Limited is India's largest private sector bank by assets and market capitalization. The bank offers a comprehensive suite of financial products and services, including wholesale, retail, and treasury operations. Its business model is diversified across retail loans, corporate banking, and third-party product distribution. As a systemically important financial institution, its performance is a key barometer for the Indian economy. The bank is professionally managed with a 0.0% promoter holding and a 100% public float, leading to high institutional ownership.
