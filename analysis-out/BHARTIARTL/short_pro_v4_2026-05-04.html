About

Bharti Airtel Limited is a leading global telecommunications company with operations in 18 countries across Asia and Africa. Its primary business is providing mobile, fixed-line, and broadband services, as well as digital TV and mobile commerce. As a large-cap, blue-chip entity with a market capitalization of ₹1,149,222 Cr, it is a core holding in the Indian telecom sector. The stock is currently trading at ₹1887, which is approximately 33% up from its 52-week low, indicating a recovery phase but not yet in overextended territory. The company's business model is driven by subscriber growth, Average Revenue Per User (ARPU), and data consumption trends, with a significant focus on the rollout and monetization of its 5G network.
