About

Bajaj Finance Limited is one of India's leading Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFC), primarily engaged in lending. Its business model spans consumer finance (personal loans, durable finance), SME and commercial lending, and wealth management. With a market capitalization of ₹5,83,123 Cr, it is a large-cap stalwart in the financial services sector. The stock is currently trading at ₹937.0, positioned in the middle of its 52-week range of ₹788 to ₹1102, indicating a period of consolidation after significant price swings.

Fundamentals

For a short-term horizon, the most relevant fundamental signals come from the latest quarterly performance, which sets near-term market expectations. In its latest quarter (Mar 2026), Bajaj Finance reported a Net Profit of ₹5,553 Cr and an EPS of ₹8.78. This represents strong year-over-year growth of 22.2% in profit, a positive signal for earnings momentum. Quarterly revenue stood at ₹21,606 Cr, showing a sequential increase of 2.8% over the previous quarter, indicating steady business expansion. While the company's balance sheet shows high borrowings, a characteristic of the lending industry, the consistent profit growth has historically supported investor confidence. The key takeaway is that the company's earnings trajectory remains strong, providing a solid fundamental backdrop for the stock's near-term performance.

The cash flow statement reveals a critical aspect of an NBFC's operations. The large and persistent negative Cash from Operating Activities (OCF) is due to the nature of the business, where loan disbursements (an increase in assets) are treated as a cash outflow. While Net Profit is strong at ₹19,332 Cr, the OCF is ₹-65,790 Cr. The business funds this gap through Cash from Financing Activities (raising debt/equity). This structure is normal for a growing lender but highlights its dependence on continuous access to capital markets.
