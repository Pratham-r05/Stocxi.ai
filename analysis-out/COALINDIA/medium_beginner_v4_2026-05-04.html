COAL INDIA LIMITED (NSE: COALINDIA)

Analysis for: Next 3–12 Months

About

Coal India Limited is a government-owned company and is the single largest coal producer in the world. Think of it as the main supplier of coal for India's power plants, which generate the electricity we use every day. Because it's so large (with a market value of ₹296,643 Cr) and crucial to the country's energy needs, it's considered a very stable and established company. People often invest in Coal India not just for business growth, but because it has a history of sharing its profits with investors through payments called dividends. It is a foundational company in the Indian economy's energy sector.

Fundamentals

For the next 3 to 12 months, the health of the company's business is the most important factor. The business seems to be doing well, but there are mixed signals to watch. While its sales are growing, the profit for the full year has recently dipped. However, the most recent three-month period showed strong improvement. The main thing that will affect the stock in the next year is the demand for coal, which often rises during hot summers when more electricity is needed for air conditioning.

Here’s a simple breakdown of the company's financial health:

  • Revenue (Sales): Think of this as the total money the company collected from its customers. In the last full year (ending March 2026), it collected ₹168,400 Cr. This shows the large scale of its operations.
  • Profit: After paying for all its expenses like salaries, equipment, and taxes, the company was left with a profit of ₹31,071 Cr for the year. This is the actual earnings of the business.
  • Margins (Efficiency): This tells us how efficient the company is. For every ₹100 Coal India made from sales, it earned about ₹28 in operating profit. This is a very healthy efficiency level and is a positive sign.
  • Debt: The company has borrowed ₹14,072 Cr. While this sounds like a lot, it's very small compared to the company's own net worth. Its debt-to-equity ratio is just 0.12, which is very comfortable and low-risk, like having a tiny mortgage on a very valuable property.

Financial Statements

Looking at the company's recent performance and financial position gives us a clearer picture.

In the last three months of the financial year (January to March 2026), the company made ₹46,490 Cr in sales, which was a significant jump from the ₹34,924 Cr it made in the previous three months. Its profit also grew strongly to ₹10,908 Cr in the same period. This shows that the business momentum is currently improving, which is a positive sign for the year ahead.

The company's balance sheet, which is a snapshot of what it owns and what it owes, is very strong.

  • The company owns assets worth ₹285,660 Cr and has total borrowings of only ₹14,072 Cr. Its net value, or equity, is very high, providing a strong financial cushion.
  • Its ability to pay back debt is excellent. The company earns more than 33 times the amount it needs to pay in interest on its loans, which means there is almost no risk of it being unable to manage its debt.
  • Most importantly, the company is generating a lot of real cash from its business. Its operating cash flow for the year was ₹43,215 Cr, which is significantly higher than its net profit. This is a sign of very high-quality earnings.

Overall Summary & AI Opinion

  • Overall medium-term picture: Coal India is a financially very healthy and stable company. The business has shown strong improvement in the most recent quarter, and the stock price has been rising to reflect this positive outlook.
