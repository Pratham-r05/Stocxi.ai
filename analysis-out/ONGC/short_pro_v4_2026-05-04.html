About

Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC) is a large-cap public sector undertaking operating in the oil exploration and production sector. With a market capitalization of ₹376,591 Cr, it is a dominant player in India's energy landscape. The company's operations are capital-intensive, reflected in its substantial fixed asset base and ongoing capital work-in-progress. A significant promoter holding by the Government of India (58.9%) provides stability, while a public float of 41.1% ensures adequate liquidity in the market. The business model is inherently tied to global commodity cycles, influencing revenue and profitability.
