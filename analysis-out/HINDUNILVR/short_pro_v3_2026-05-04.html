About

Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) is a large-cap stalwart in the Indian Diversified FMCG sector, with a market capitalization of ₹528,799 Cr. The company operates across multiple consumer product categories, including home care, beauty & personal care, and foods & refreshment. With a high promoter holding of 61.9%, the company demonstrates strong management conviction. As a blue-chip entity, its performance is often considered a bellwether for consumer demand and economic sentiment in India.

  • Quarterly Earnings: The latest quarterly EPS for the period ending March 2026 was ₹12.73. This represents a significant sequential decline of 54.7% from the ₹28.12 reported in the December 2025 quarter. Similarly, Net Profit fell 54.7% QoQ from ₹6,603 Cr to ₹2,994 Cr.

  • Balance Sheet Strength: The company maintains a robust balance sheet with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of just 0.03. This near-zero leverage provides significant financial stability and minimizes risk, a strong positive backdrop even amidst slowing operational momentum.

While the long-term profitability metrics like ROE (30.9%) and annual OPM (23.0%) remain strong, the sharp sequential drop in quarterly profit and revenue stagnation are the most critical fundamental signals for the immediate 30-90 day outlook.
